The American Medical College of Homeopathy (AMCofH) has created a Homeopathic Practitioner program is a three-year, full-time program that fully prepares our students for national certification. This course offers classroom training, clinical training, and business courses to give our students a well-rounded homeopathic education that prepares them to run their own practice. AMCofH values clinical experience, which is why we offer our students over 500 hours of clinical training with over 100 patient contacts. Our students have the opportunity to discuss cases and ask questions to a certified homeopath with our student mentorship program.

Live and Online

EXPLORE THE PIHMA CLINIC

AMCofH allows its students to take individual cases with supervision which ties together all of their homeopathic training. We feel that strong clinical training brings students homeopathic education to life and is critical for the success of our future homeopaths.

Acute Care is for new or recent situations causing a complaint. Complaints may include sprains, food poisoning, coughs, colds, indigestion, bruises, bites, burns, diarrhea, ear infections, and more. Appointments last 15-30 minutes.

Constitutional Care is for chronic problems such as grief, PTSD, arthritis, IBS, infertility, asthma, eczema, and more. Appointments go in-depth into the patient’s health and last up to 2 hours.

REQUIREMENTS:

All applicants must possess an Associate’s degree with a minimum GPA of 2.5 in all post-secondary work. For exceptions, please click here.

Non-medically licensed applicants must also successfully complete the following course work either during or prior to the program at an accredited or approved college:

  • Anatomy: 3-semester credits (33 hours of lecture)
  • Physiology: 3-semester credits (33 hours of lecture)
  • Pathophysiology: 4-semester credits (40 hours of lecture)
  • CPR Training

Prerequisite courses taken over seven years ago are subject to review. Classes can be completed prior to or concurrent with the program, as co-requisites.

Off-campus students enrolled in the Homeopathic Practitioner Program are required to have a computer with high-speed internet access and basic computer familiarity.

IMPORTANT QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS:

Do you have financial aid?

AMCofH does not offer financial aid. However, payment plans are available!

Are classes online?
AMCofH classes are all online. Some are to be done at your own pace and some are live (with a teacher and other classmates). Clinical hours must be done in person.

Will this program make me a certified homeopath?

This program will prepare you to be CHC certified. After completing the program, you will need to pass the national certification exam. Once you pass the exam you will be a certified homeopath.

Before you shop for medicines…

To use homeopathic medicines more effectively, it is IMPORTANT to know that these natural medicines work most effectively when they are prescribed based on the sick person's unique pattern (or syndrome) of symptoms, not just the “name” of their disease.

Secondly, homeopathic medicines are not like vitamins or herbs. Generally, it is not necessary to take them every day (except when instructed to do so by a competent homeopath). Sometimes, only a couple of doses a day for 1 to 3 days is enough. The idea is to take as little as possible but as much as necessary. Remember, these remedies are “catalysts” to your body's self-healing capabilities.

If you want to treat minor and acute illnesses or injuries

  • Your results will be more effective and spectacular IF you learn how to individualize a specific homeopathic medicine based upon the sick person's overall symptoms (their “syndrome”).
  • It is best if you purchase an introductory book that teaches you which medicine to use.
