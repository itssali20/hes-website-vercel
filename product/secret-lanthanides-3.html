The Lanthanides are elements of the periodic system with atom number 57 till 73. The have strong electro-magnetic and light properties.

Autonomy is a major theme, hence the subtitle “Road to independence.”

The search for freedom can lead to a spiritual development and desire for self-control. The Lanthanides are needed for leading ones own life, going one’s own way. It’s the road to individuality and individuation.

The psychological separation is physically reflected in the immune system and auto-immune diseases. The immune system separates own tissue from foreign substances, it has to find out what “own”.

It’s hardly possible to overstress the importance of the Lanthanides. Although they look obscure and hardly important as remedies, their place is unique and cannot be missed. In 10 years time they will be standard in homeopathy.

The working of the Lanthanides is hidden, as the elements themselves are hidden. That’s why they were called rare earth elements. But they are less rare than, for instance, silver, gold or iodine. It’s only that they were hidden from the surface of the world by mixing themselves with all kinds of elements.

The Lanthanides have a very important place in homeopathy. They have a special connection with all the auto-immune diseases. These diseases were difficult to treat in the past, and the Lanthanides form a welcome addition in the homeopathic Materia Medica.

Before you shop for medicines…

To use homeopathic medicines more effectively, it is IMPORTANT to know that these natural medicines work most effectively when they are prescribed based on the sick person's unique pattern (or syndrome) of symptoms, not just the “name” of their disease.

Secondly, homeopathic medicines are not like vitamins or herbs. Generally, it is not necessary to take them every day (except when instructed to do so by a competent homeopath). Sometimes, only a couple of doses a day for 1 to 3 days is enough. The idea is to take as little as possible but as much as necessary. Remember, these remedies are “catalysts” to your body's self-healing capabilities.

  • Your results will be more effective and spectacular IF you learn how to individualize a specific homeopathic medicine based upon the sick person's overall symptoms (their “syndrome”).
