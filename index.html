Dilution & potency

Reading a label

6C12C30C200CLM

Arnica montana 30C — the medicine name in Latin, then its potency. Higher numbers are more dilute, not larger doses. Most home use starts at 30C.

Homeopathy is a 200-year-old system of medicine that uses very small doses of natural substances to stimulate the body's own healing response. A remedy is chosen because, in larger amounts, it would produce symptoms similar to the ones the person has — the principle of "like cures like". It is not herbalism and not a supplement: the medicines are prepared by repeated dilution and succussion, and are regulated as drugs in the United States. Read the full guide →
You match the remedy to the whole picture of symptoms, not just the diagnosis. Two people with the same cold may need different medicines depending on how they feel — restless or sluggish, thirsty or not, better in fresh air or worse. Our Shop by Health Concern pages narrow it down for you, and Everybody's Guide to Homeopathic Medicines walks you through it step by step. Read the full guide →
Potency is the number on the label — 6C, 12C, 30C, 200C or LM. Higher numbers are more dilute, not stronger doses. For everyday home use, 30C is the usual starting point; 6C and 12C suit gentler or more frequent dosing, and 200C is generally for experienced users and practitioners. Read the full guide →

Repeat while symptoms are improving, and stop once you are better; there is no benefit in continuing. If nothing changes after a few doses, the remedy is probably not the right match. Before you shop for medicines…

To use homeopathic medicines more effectively, it is IMPORTANT to know that these natural medicines work most effectively when they are prescribed based on the sick person's unique pattern (or syndrome) of symptoms, not just the “name” of their disease.

Secondly, homeopathic medicines are not like vitamins or herbs. Generally, it is not necessary to take them every day (except when instructed to do so by a competent homeopath). Sometimes, only a couple of doses a day for 1 to 3 days is enough. The idea is to take as little as possible but as much as necessary. Remember, these remedies are “catalysts” to your body's self-healing capabilities.

If you want to treat minor and acute illnesses or injuries

  • Your results will be more effective and spectacular IF you learn how to individualize a specific homeopathic medicine based upon the sick person's overall symptoms (their “syndrome”).
  • It is best if you purchase an introductory book that teaches you which medicine to use.
